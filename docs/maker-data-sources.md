# Where the sample maker data comes from

The **people and groups are invented** (names, quotes, prices, years): no real person or business is named, because naming someone needs their agreement. The **crafts, materials and processes** are based on published sources, so the samples are true to the place.

| Sample | What it is based on | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Maua Weavers' Circle (vidasi baskets) | Taita sisal baskets, called *vidasi*, are woven by women, a skill passed from mother or grandmother to daughter; baskets were once given as wedding gifts; sisal is dried, hand-rolled into twine and dyed; weaving starts at the centre of the base | WIPO "Taita Basket: A New Identity for Basket Weavers in Kenya"; Kenya News Agency "Taita Taveta basket weavers to access global market"; One Village One Product Kenya (iovop.org) |
| Basket groups in general | A "Taita Baskets Association" was registered in 2016 (about 400 members) with a collective mark; the county holds a craft market on the first Saturday of each month for baskets, bead-work and leather | WIPO; Kenya News Agency |
| Mwatate Bead Sisters (beadwork), leatherworks | Women's groups in the county make bead-work and leather goods; groups of Maa women do bead-work | Kenya News Agency (craft market days) |
| Dawida Hive Co-operative (honey) | Community beekeeping and agroforestry in Taita Taveta, hives at the edge of farmland and wildlife areas, training in colony health and honey traceability | RFI / Capmad report (July 2026); Kenya News Agency on beekeeping training |
| Voi Gem Bench (tsavorite) | Tsavorite, first identified in the Tsavo region in 1967, is found in Taita Taveta and is promoted as a uniquely Kenyan gemstone; Mwatate is one of the gem-producing areas | Skyjems encyclopedia (Mwatate); The Star / People Daily on the tsavorite plans; Kenya News Agency |
| Teita Sisal Estate | One of the largest sisal estates in the world, near Mwatate | Wikipedia |

Positions are placed in or near each town and are approximate. Prices are plausible sample prices, not market research.

# Organisations you could approach as sponsors or partners

These are **prospects, not partners**. Nothing here is shown on the site, and no relationship is implied. Each appears in the research for the stories; check current details and contact them yourself.

| Organisation | Why it fits | Where it appeared in the research |
|---|---|---|

| Kenya Tourism Board (Magical Kenya) | Promotes Tsavo and Taita Taveta | magicalkenya.com |
| Nature Kenya (East Africa Natural History Society) | Works on the Taita Hills forests and their birds | Google Arts & Culture, Taita stories |
| National Museums of Kenya | Holds Taita cultural heritage; produced "Shujaa Stories" | Google Arts & Culture |
| Commonwealth War Graves Commission | Cares for the Taveta, Maktau and Voi war graves | CWGC records |

| Wildlife Works | Community conservation around Mount Kasigau | Wildlife Works blog (Kasigau) |

Approach each with something specific: a story featuring them, an event, a package from the sponsors page.
